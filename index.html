Four steps, every week

  1. HoldGig payouts land in a buffer, not straight in checking. A share is set aside for tax.
  2. ForecastA model predicts a low, middle and high case for next week’s take-home pay.
  3. CalibrateThe low case is adjusted until it is right about as often as it claims.
  4. PayKeel pays the highest paycheck that keeps the chance of an empty buffer under your limit.
