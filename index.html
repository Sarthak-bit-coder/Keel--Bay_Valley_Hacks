Methodology

Four steps to a steadier paycheck.

Keel separates what you spend each week from what a client or platform happened to pay you that week.

  1. edit_noteStep 01Log weekly earningsRecord what you were paid each week, or explore realistic simulated weeks. No bank or platform logins.Input: gross pay, any job
  2. account_balanceStep 02Set aside for taxesA share you choose (15% by default, illustrative) is set aside before Keel works out the paycheck.Set-aside: 10% to 25%
  3. savingsStep 03Build a bufferGood weeks add to a buffer instead of straight to spending, so the next slow week is already covered.Surplus → buffer
  4. wavesStep 04Pay through slow weeksKeel pays the highest paycheck that keeps the chance of an empty buffer under the limit you pick.Outcome: one steady paycheck

Empirical foundation

Built on real NYC ride-hail trip data.

Instead of made-up income curves, the simulated drivers earn from the real NYC TLC High Volume For-Hire Vehicle records: real hourly pay and tips, real weather, real holidays.

scienceSimulation methodologyThree simulated drivers (full-time, part-time, weekend-only) over 52 weeks, trained and tested on time-ordered weeks that never overlap.
tuneCalibrated forecastsLightGBM forecasts a low, middle and high case. Conformal calibration adjusts the low case so it is right about as often as it claims.
functionA rule you can checkThe paycheck is the highest amount whose simulated chance of emptying the buffer stays under your limit. No hidden levers.
