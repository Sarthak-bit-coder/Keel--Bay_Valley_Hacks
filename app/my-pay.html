How this page works

Keel replays your own weeks: from week 5 on, it picks each paycheck with the same Monte Carlo rule as the main results (highest paycheck whose chance of an empty buffer in the next 4 weeks stays under your limit) and compares it with paying last week's pay or the 4-week average. The starting buffer is half a week of your average take-home pay.

One difference: the main results forecast with a LightGBM model and conformal calibration. Here the low, middle and high case are simply the 10%, 50% and 90% points of your last 12 weeks, so they are uncalibrated and less reliable, especially with few weeks. Example weeks are simulated from our own assumptions, not data about real workers.
